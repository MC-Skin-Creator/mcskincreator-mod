# MC Skin Creator — Mod changelog

## Unreleased

### New
- **Quilt support** — the mod now runs natively on Quilt, on every supported Minecraft version.
- **Forge support** — builds for Forge are now produced for every Minecraft version.
- **Many more Minecraft versions** — every release from 1.20 to 26.3 is now supported.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-09-30

### New
- **Now on CurseForge** — every stable release is also published there, next to Modrinth.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-30

### New
- **NeoForge support** — the mod now runs on NeoForge as well as Fabric, on every supported version.
- **More Minecraft versions** — 1.21.10, 26.1 and 26.3 join the supported list.

### Fixed
- Mouse buttons work again on every supported Minecraft version.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-29

### Improved
- **Your saved skins stay on your computer** — they are no longer shared across machines.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-29

### New
- **A button in the menus** — a Skin Creator button sits on the title screen and the pause menu, and turns square on small screens.
- **Your project is kept** — close the editor and reopen it, your project in progress is still there.
- **One entry per skin** — *My skins* keeps a single entry per project, which you can rename or duplicate.
- **Apply first** — the export window now leads with applying the skin to your account, which happens in the background.
- **Credits explained** — the credits of a skin are explained in plain words and come with a Copy button.
- **Button clicks** — the editor plays the game's button click when you press a button.

### Improved
- The ready-made starter sheets are drawn off the main thread, so opening them no longer stutters.
- The catalogue loads faster and uses less data, and is remembered between launches.

### Fixed
- Previewing an outfit no longer shows it on the pink default skin, and a hovered outfit appears over the skin you are wearing.
- A layer's colours can be changed from the layers panel.
- The delete button in *My skins* works, and asks you to confirm first.
- A layer no longer keeps blinking after the pointer leaves its row.
- The swing button is gone from the in-game view, and the view chooser moved into the dock.
- The picture of a saved skin refreshes when the skin changes.
- The category title is no longer repeated above a single category.
